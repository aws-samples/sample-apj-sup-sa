In Part 1 you ran a finished agent: you opened the baseline already in your
workspace, deployed it to AgentCore Runtime, and watched it investigate an anomaly
end to end. Every design decision was already made for you.

Part 2 flips that. Now **you** make the decisions. Working in teams of 2-3, you
will design your own version of the agent using the **GenAI Product Canvas**, then
hand that canvas to a coding agent to build and deploy it. The canvas is where your
design lives before it becomes code.

The three steps of Part 2:

1. **[Design](./041-design-canvas.md)** — fill in the canvas: what the agent produces, when it
   stops, where the human sits, how fast it runs, and who uses it.
2. **[Build](./050-build.md)** — translate your canvas into a working agent, validate
   it locally, and deploy it to AgentCore Runtime.
3. **[Validate](./080-validate.md)** — check your deployed agent against the scenarios
   and against your own canvas decisions.

There is no single correct canvas. Your team's answers shape the agent you build,
and you will test those answers against a running agent in the next step.
