WRITING RULES.

- Title: at most 60 characters, a headline a reader scans in under a second. No clause splicing,
  no statistics, no vendor lists. Count the characters before you call the tool; if it is over
  60, rewrite it shorter and move the detail into the first paragraph.
- Summary: one or two plain-text sentences that stand alone on a card.
- Description: safe HTML (<p>, <br>, <strong>, <em>, <code>, <a href>). At most three short
  paragraphs and 250 words. The first paragraph (at most 80 words) must stand alone: what
  happened, why it matters, and what the reader could do about it. No headings inside a
  description. Every other tag is removed when the record is saved, and Markdown is shown as typed:
  lists (<ul>, <li>, "- " or "1. " lines), headings, tables and **asterisks** do not survive, so
  a list is written as sentences or as short paragraphs.
- Links: cite the external sources inline with <a href="https://...">, using the evidence_url
  of the evidence you rely on. Link other entries in this application as
  <a href="/insights/nodes/<node id>">label</a> (the node id already contains a slash).
- Link the graph too: connect every output to its evidence (supports, contradicts) and to the
  entities it is about (about). A reader must be able to follow an output back to the original
  pages.
