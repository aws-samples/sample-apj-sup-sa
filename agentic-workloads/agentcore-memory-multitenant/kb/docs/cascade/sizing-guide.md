# Cascade Mart sizing guide

## Clothing
Cascade Mart basics use unisex sizing from XS to 3XL, cut slightly relaxed. Most shoppers take their usual size.

## Chest measurements
M fits 38-40 in, L fits 42-44 in, XL fits 46-48 in.

## Kids
Kids' sizes are listed by age (2T to 14) rather than by measurement.
