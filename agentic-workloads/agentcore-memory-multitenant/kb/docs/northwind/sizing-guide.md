# Northwind Apparel Co. sizing guide

## Fit
Northwind clothing uses our Classic Fit, which runs true to size. If you are between two sizes, choose the larger size.

## Tops and jackets
Chest measurements: S fits 35-37 in, M fits 38-40 in, L fits 41-43 in, XL fits 44-46 in.

## Trousers
Trousers come in waist sizes 28 to 40 and three leg lengths: short (30 in), regular (32 in) and long (34 in).
